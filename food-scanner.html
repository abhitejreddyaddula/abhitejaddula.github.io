    How the score works

    Every product gets a score from 0 to 100:

    • Excellent
      75–100
    • Good
      50–74
    • Poor
      25–49
    • Bad
      0–24

    60% nutrition (based on the Nutri-Score method), 30% additives, 10% organic label. A product containing a high-risk additive can't score above 49.

    Results

    Product data from Open Food Facts (Open Database License). Scores are a simplified estimate for comparing products, not medical or dietary advice.
